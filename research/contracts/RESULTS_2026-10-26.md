The shear certificate uses only `propext` and `Quot.sound`. The adapter uses no
axioms and its elaborated proof body uses both recorded premise binders (indices
6 and 7). The allowlist explicitly permits only `propext`, `Classical.choice`,
and `Quot.sound`; the rational interface fixture additionally uses choice.

Final raw evidence is in
`../runs/procedure-contract-check-20261026-d/`. The compact tracked inventory
`check_results_2026-10-26.json` contains all attempt locations, exact source,
checker and toolchain hashes, per-case timings, axiom lists, interface hashes,
comparison results, and test-log hash. It inventories local artifacts; it does
not claim they were uploaded or backed up.

Failures remain visible. Attempt `-a` failed because `lean -o` required an
explicit package root. Attempt `-b` failed because Lean 4.9 lacks `List.idxOf`;
two reported negative-case flags from that broken prelude are excluded from
the evidence. Attempt `-c` passed, then was superseded by `-d` after making
kernel-comparison exceptions fail closed and adding provenance hashes. One
initial unittest readback failed because a class attribute shadowed
`TestCase.run`; renaming it fixed both subsequent readbacks. Across all four
attempts there were 20 fixture invocations and four version queries, including
the failed work. No attempt timed out.

This instrument handles already closed theorem certificates. It does not
extract arbitrary local `have` blocks, certify shared-metavariable cuts, or
equate arbitrary semantic interfaces. The alpha hash is scoped to the recorded
environment; provenance does not hash every transitive olean. Imported oleans
are trusted as in the production runtime. Production-wrapper agreement and
persistent REPL replay are separate checks. No frozen transfer tasks or
witnesses were read or used in these fixtures. See `README.md` for the precise
contract, frame, exclusions, and reproduction command.

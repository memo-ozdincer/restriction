# Recursive exclusion of concrete witnesses

Can a frozen recursive-GRPO actor produce a different verified integer solution after its dominant witness is explicitly forbidden? Use positive ordered legs of a right triangle with hypotenuse 65. This tiny finite problem has four admissible witnesses. Ordering removes swaps and positivity removes the zero solution. The actor sees the theorem, a standard request to give explicit numerical witnesses, and any active inequalities. It sees no solution list. No training.

Run 16 draws for discovery, then up to two matched comparisons of 16 draws each, using the same seed at a given depth for ordinary and exclusion prompts. Promote the uniquely most frequent identified accepted witness when it occurs at least twice and comprises at least 40% of accepted attempts. Stop at a failed dominance gate. A witness is identified only when the original proof verifies and its literal constructor pair passes the independent arithmetic and active exclusions. Record every attempt and any verified unclassified proof. The outcome is whether hard exclusion adds a verified witness absent from all same-budget control groups, and how it changes the frequency of accepted alternatives on each round. The intervention changes the inference-time specification. Its result measures prompted discovery, separately from training transfer.

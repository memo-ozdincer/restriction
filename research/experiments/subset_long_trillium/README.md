# Long subset-sum learning: registered Trillium CPU panel

## Hypothesis and decision (registered before submission)

The original fixed 20-item unique-solution subset-sum panel found one rare delayed breakthrough (seed 49, annealed tolerance: two relaxed positive rewards, first exact hit at update 480, 19.92% final analytic exact probability), with every other policy below 0.006%. The ambiguity is whether the apparently stagnant policies simply need time to encounter their first or next exact success, or whether assistance trained them into distributions that have effectively eliminated the exact solution. The original 600 updates (153,600 candidate draws) give a uniform policy only ~0.146 expected exact hits; absence of early successes alone is weak evidence of collapse.

**Hypothesis:** extending the same learning trajectories 100-fold (60,000 updates, 15,360,000 candidate draws per condition) distinguishes persistent stagnation/collapse from delayed self-reinforcing exact discoveries. A uniform policy would encounter ~14.65 exact solutions in that budget. Inspect exact solution probability analytically over time, before and after first actual exact rewards, rather than interpreting an all-zero evaluation sample as zero policy support. Test whether late crutch removal is preferable to the original schedule or sustained tolerance/mask use by comparing matched forks at update 600. If ordinary training catches up, the earlier positive result is primarily a discovery-time effect; if assistance produces more/higher exact-success runs despite long ordinary training, pursue the intervention while auditing objective changes; if relaxed policies' exact probability collapses, avoid treating more relaxed rewards as progress.

- Fixed task, exact verifier, common product Bernoulli learner, RNG stream layout, initialization, Adam hyperparameters, batch 256, original `TOL=16384`, unchanged original transition schedules and first 600 updates. All original eight paired seeds 42..49; no seed selection or additional seed replication. Each run is regenerated from the original initialization through update 600 to preserve parameters **and optimizer moments/RNG state**, then extended with the same schedule. The already-verified original result records are checked exactly at 600 and at every earlier checkpoint, including logits.
- Original six arms: exact, sustained tolerance, tolerance switch at 180, tolerance anneal to exact by 180, random-bit-mask switch at 180, mask anneal to exact by 180. Three targeted additional arms: tolerance sustained until update 600 then exact; random six-bit mask sustained throughout; same six-bit mask sustained until 600 then exact. The late-switch arms share the exact same first-600 trajectories as their sustained counterparts. The sustained six-bit mask draws a fresh mask per batch using the same original mask mechanism and independent RNG. No new optimizer or policy modifications.
- 9×8 = 72 runs, 60,000 updates each, 1,105,920,000 whole-candidate training proposals total (15,360,000 per run). All conditions receive identical candidate budgets. Independent evaluation at every original 30-update interval through update 600, then every 300 updates through 60,000, always 2,048 fresh samples per evaluation. Logged checkpoint logits permit exact solution probability calculated **offline** using independently enumerated unique subset; never supplied to learning loop. Track first exact hit step, batch exact/reward hits, cumulative hits, max absolute logits and final parameters. No adaptive stop or best-seed selection. Use exact probability >=10% as descriptive threshold, not a hypothesis-test cutoff. Analyze paired per-seed outcomes and distinguish training discovery from sustained final probability. No generalization claim.
- Preregistered decision: durable high exact probability in continued exact arms indicates budget alone can discover the fixed solution; early vs late switched forks with higher rates indicate different timing/reward accessibility; sustained relaxation with high relaxed reward but vanishing exact probability indicates mismatch. For a run with no exact hits after 60k, use the logged analytic exact probability to distinguish mere sampling drought from effective support collapse.
- This is a bounded static whole-candidate optimization problem, not an LLM experiment; temporary tolerance and bit masking alter the training checker and introduce implicit objective shaping (see source report), so results are not interchangeable with unchanged-verifier native-state reset experiments. Per-mode reward computations differ slightly in cost, though full candidates and update counts are matched.

## Findings: ordinary RL catches up, sustained relaxation collapses

All 8 original exact-only policies converge to the unique solution. The sparse baseline is a discovery-time bottleneck, not an observed persistent inability to learn. The early crutch variants and late bit switch also reach the same saturation. Late tolerance switch converges only in 3/8; sustained tolerance and sustained random-bit masks converge in 0/8 and overwhelmingly suppress the actual solution. Earlier exact hits can occur during assistance without producing lasting exact probability. The ordinary policy already reaches all 8/8 above 90% by update 7,200, just 12 times the original 600-step budget. Annealed tolerance reaches 10% earlier in some seeds but delays others; final advantage is absent. Early tolerance switch discovers exact candidates earlier in median but reaches high exact probability later than ordinary in median. Late bit switch often sees exact candidates during relaxed training (therefore first discovery is not equivalent to first reinforced exact reward), yet convergence is slower. Sustained relaxations reduce mean log10 exact support to -64.79 (tolerance) and -56.81 (bits), despite many relaxed rewards. Late tolerance switches preserve collapsed support in five seeds; three eventually escape. All final saturated policies gave 4,096/4,096 fresh independent valid candidates. The 3/8 late tolerance condition likewise yields 12,288/32,768 fresh total successes; sustained conditions yield 0/32,768 each.

Decision: retain the rare initial anneal result as useful evidence that the interventions can change discovery timing, but the longer ordinary baseline resolves the apparent capability gap on this fixed task. This is not evidence to scale subset-sum relaxation as a superior method, or continue adding seeds purely for significance. If developing it further, investigate the explicit interaction between probability mass on incompatible valid relaxed solutions and switch timing, rather than blindly extending training. No generalization to independent subset-sum instances is claimed.

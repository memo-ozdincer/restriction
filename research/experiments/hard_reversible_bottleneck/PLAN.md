## CNOT-only intermediate family (registered before its training)

The long minimum-path three-wire panel is null: many assisted successes but zero unassisted root success, with support collapse under most exposures. This motivates testing a familiar structured subclass where local skill is more likely reusable, rather than extending failed arms for significance. Restrict to CNOT gates on five wires (20 possible ordered control/target pairs). States are invertible binary 5×5 matrices and one action XORs a control row into target row; exact goal matrix equality is equivalent to agreement on every possible input word. Choose the first random 10-step legal walk, seed 20261128, certified distance ten by root depth5 and goal depth4 no-intersection. This is a new fixed task independent of learning outcomes.

Limited pilot: 4 paired seeds, 6000 updates, batch64, horizon10. Compare two mathematically equivalent policy observation encodings: raw row masks of the current matrix (one-hot 5×32), and residual column masks of goal·current^-1 (one-hot 5×32). The latter is derivable directly from state+goal and is a fixed invertible coordinate change, not a solution path or distance oracle; action vocabulary/reward remain identical, and it is offered to both root-only and assisted arms as an independent factor. No algorithmic step recommendations or oracle feedback. Exact shortest-path DAG is computed separately and used only offline to measure otherwise inaccessible root probabilities. Identical optimizer and action budget across all arms. This tests whether task structure plus neutral reset distribution can produce a root breakthrough under zero ordinary reward; if neither encoding works, report null without extended budget.
